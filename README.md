# D-Mart-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
## Project-Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze D-Mart sales and profitability data using Microsoft Excel and develop an interactive dashboard that provides clear insights into sales trends, category-wise sales and profit, regional profit margins, and city-wise sales performance. The project focuses on using PivotTables, Excel formulas, charts, KPIs, and interactive slicers to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Questions & KPIs

### Questions Answered by the Dashboard

**1. What are the total sales and total profit generated by D-Mart?**  
**KPI:** Total Sales and Total Profit

**2. How do sales change throughout the year?**  
**KPI:** Monthly Sales Trend

**3. Which product category generates the highest sales?**  
**KPI:** Category-wise Sales

**4. Which product category generates the highest profit?**  
**KPI:** Category-wise Profit

**5. Which region has the highest share of profit margin?**  
**KPI:** Regional Profit Margin %

**6. Which cities contribute the most to D-Mart's sales?**  
**KPI:** City-wise Sales

**7. How does sales performance vary across different cities and regions?**  
**KPI:** City-wise and Region-wise Sales
